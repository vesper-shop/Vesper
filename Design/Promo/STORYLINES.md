# Promo storylines

Three candidate films for the Vesper promo (35–45 s, music and effects, no voiceover). One gets
picked and written up as `SPEC.md`, shot by shot, before any composition is built.

## A. Evening Star (recommended)

- **Device / form**: a day passing; the app's appearance follows the light.
- **What the viewer sees and feels**: one day kept in Vesper. Morning on paper-white: a memo is
  written and pinned. Afternoon: a photo goes up to Moment and lands in the timeline. Dusk: the
  system turns dark, Vesper turns with it, and Atlas opens on the map with the day's pins. The
  name lands last, as the evening star.
- **First two seconds**: the splash wordmark gathering in on paper, then the Memos feed settling
  out of its skeleton.
- **Beats**: wordmark → morning memo (compose, #tag suggestion, pin with the star appearing) →
  afternoon Moment (camera, the ThumbHash blur sharpening into the photo, the timeline chip) →
  dusk: light to dark on one cut → Atlas map, pins dropping, the calendar → end card "Vesper",
  "Memos, Moment and Atlas, in one place".
- **Look**: the app's own screens only; the paper palette (`#FAF7EB` / `#1A1917`, brand
  `#963C5A` / `#D8A0B2`) for every ground between shots; SF Pro titles, set left, never centred
  on a glass card.
- **Sound**: one calm track that opens up at dusk; the app's haptic moments (pin, post) as soft
  ticks on the beat.

## B. Liquid Glass

- **Device / form**: the material as the hook; macro shots cut on the beat.
- **What the viewer sees and feels**: the camera sits close on the glass: the tag bar sliding
  under the thumb, the toast rising, the tab bar folding away on scroll, the pinned deck
  springing open, a photo's blur filling the detail page.
- **First two seconds**: a full-frame macro of the tag bar, a chip turning accent on the downbeat.
- **Beats**: tag bar → pinned deck opens → search slides down → photo grid pinch (lanes change on
  the beat) → detail page blur → map locate and back to Britain → title.
- **Look**: punch-ins of 2–3x on the recordings; hard cuts on bar lines; no added glow.
- **Sound**: a percussive track; each cut is a beat.

## C. One Sign-In

- **Device / form**: the argument carried by type; one sign-in opens three sites.
- **What the viewer sees and feels**: big words over the real screens: "One sign-in." Settings,
  Sign in with you-find.me, the account card fills in; then "Memos." "Moment." "Atlas." each
  word cutting to its tab already loaded.
- **First two seconds**: the word "One" alone on paper, then the Settings account row.
- **Beats**: sign in → three words, three tabs → the iPhone Duo unfolding to the wide layout →
  the Mac window → end card.
- **Look**: large SF Pro words in brand colour over the recordings, hard colour fields from the
  palette between them.
- **Sound**: a track with three hits under the three names.

## Assumptions

- 40 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps; a 1080×1920 cut-down for vertical feeds can come from the same SPEC.
- Every screen is the app itself, recorded from the iOS Simulator (`footage/README.md`), never
  redrawn or mocked in HTML.
- Recordings use a demo set of memos and photos, not personal ones (see `footage/README.md`).
- Copy comes from the app and its README; no claim the app does not make.
